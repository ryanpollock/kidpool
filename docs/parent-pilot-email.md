# Parent pilot email

Draft of the invitation email to send to parents for the Carpool Crew pilot.

---

**Subject: Carpool Crew — join the Clarendon/Presidio carpool pilot**

Hi everyone,

I've been building an app to help coordinate carpooling between Midtown Terrace and Presidio Middle School. It's called Carpool Crew, and I'd like to invite you to try it for a two-week pilot starting the week of August 10.

**What it does:** Each week, you check in which days your child needs a ride and which days you can drive. The app builds a schedule, assigns drivers and riders to cars, and sends confirmations. You see who's driving, who's in the car, and the pickup times — all in one place. No more group-text chains or last-minute scrambling.

**Typical drive times:** Morning pickup is 8:40 AM from Midtown Terrace. Afternoon pickup is 5:15 PM from Presidio.

**Watch a 2-minute demo:** https://www.loom.com/share/c96116a81d114510b16edf4982e2c00a

**Getting started (about 5 minutes):**
1. Go to **carpoolcrew.co** and sign in with your Google account
2. Enter your name and phone number
3. Create a household (or join one if another adult in your family already signed up)
4. Add your children
5. Add a vehicle if you plan to drive (label + number of child seats)
6. Set your typical week — which days your child needs rides and which days you can drive

**Household setup:** Each adult signs in with their **own** Google account. If you're the first in your family to sign up, create a household — the app gives you a 10-character code. Share that code with your co-parent, nanny, or any other adult who helps with pickup. They sign in separately and enter the code to join your household. A household can have two or more drivers — everyone shares the same children and vehicle, but each person has their own profile, gets their own notifications, and confirms their own driving assignments independently.

**The weekly rhythm:**
- **Saturday:** Check in on the Check-in tab — toggle which days your child needs rides and which days you can drive. Takes about a minute once your defaults are set.
- **Sunday:** I'll generate the draft schedule, then publish it. If you're assigned to drive, you'll get a confirmation request on the Home tab. Tap "Confirm all drives" or review and decline individual trips.
- **During the week:** Open the Week tab to see who's driving each day, who's in each car, and pickup times. You can add drives to your phone's calendar from the Home tab.

**Pilot timeline:**
- **Week of Aug 10 (pilot):** We'll run the full workflow — check in, generate the schedule, confirm drives — but this is a test week. No actual driving required; just walk through the app so we can shake out any issues before it's real.
- **Week of Aug 17 (first real schedule):** Real carpooling begins. Note: Monday Aug 17 is the first day of school (no carpool that day). First real carpool trips are Tuesday Aug 18 through Friday Aug 21.

**Privacy:** The app uses Google sign-in so only real, identifiable parents can join. Your household's children, vehicle, and schedule are visible only to other families in our carpool group. Your phone number and email are shared by default but you can turn that off in Account settings. No live location is tracked. You can export or delete your household data at any time.

**Questions?** There's a FAQ tab in the app, or just reply to this email.

Thanks,
Ryan
